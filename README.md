欢迎来到厦马 SAIR 生存指南！本仓库面向厦门大学马来西亚分校人工智能与机器人学院（School of Artificial Intelligence and Robotics, SAIR）的三个本科专业：电子信息工程（EEE）、人工智能（AIT）、机器人与自动化工程（ROBO）。这些专业课程强度较大、考试较为频繁、学习资源相对有限，本仓库旨在为大家建立一个开放的学习资料共享平台。通过汇集同学们的学习笔记、实验经验、考试复习资料与重点总结，并整理教师教学风格与学习建议，实现资源互助与经验传承，帮助更多同学高效学习、顺利备考，取得好成绩，并为未来申请实习与心仪 offer 打下坚实基础。

Welcome to the XMUM SAIR Survival Guide! This repo serves the three undergraduate programmes of XMUM's School of Artificial Intelligence and Robotics (SAIR): Electrical & Electronic Engineering (EEE), Artificial Intelligence (AIT) and Robotics & Automation Engineering (ROBO). They are among the heaviest programmes on campus: lots of courses, frequent exams, and not many shared resources. This repo is an open, student-run place to pool notes, lab experience, revision material and exam-prep summaries, plus honest notes on lecturers' teaching styles and study advice. The goal: help each other study smarter, get through exams with good grades, and build a solid base for internships and that dream offer.

## 初衷 | Why this exists

- 在厦马，往年试卷和笔记一直在私下小圈子里流传，甚至被拿来倒卖。
- 能拿到的同学复习有方向，没花钱、没门路的同学只能硬扛——同一门课，起跑线却不一样。
- 我们觉得学习资料不该是付费特权。这里的一切都免费、公开，任何人都能看、都能用。
- 教育公平，从一份公开的空白试卷开始。

- At XMUM, past papers and notes have long circulated in private circles, and some have even been sold between students.
- Students who have them know what to expect; students who did not (or could not) pay are left guessing. Same course, unequal starting line.
- We believe study material should not be a paid privilege. Everything here is free and open to everyone.
- Educational equity can start with something as simple as a public blank past paper.

## 仓库结构 | Repository layout

按专业 → 届（入学年-毕业年）→ 学年 → 学期 → 课程组织，不同届的资料分开存放。目前资料以 EEE 为主，AIT 和 ROBO 的位置已预留，等同学们加入。

Organised by programme → cohort (intake-graduation year) → year → semester → course, so each cohort's material stays separate. Most material so far is EEE; AIT and ROBO folders are ready and waiting for contributors.

```
README.md
DISCLAIMER.md
CONTRIBUTING.md
CONTRIBUTORS.md
Lecturers.md               (教师档案 | lecturer profiles)
Research-Opportunities.md  (研究机会 | research openings)
EEE/
  README.md
  2021-2025/          (2021 入学、2025 届 | 2021 intake, Class of 2025)
    Year-1/ … Year-4/ → Semester-1/2/3 → one folder per course
  2022-2026/          (预留 | reserved)
  2023-2027/
  2024-2028/
  2025-2029/
AIT/README.md       (人工智能 | Artificial Intelligence)
ROBO/README.md      (机器人与自动化工程 | Robotics & Automation Engineering)
```

每门课一个文件夹，例如 `EEE/2021-2025/Year-1/Semester-3/COM107_Circuits_and_Devices/`，里面按需包含：

Each course has its own folder, e.g. `EEE/2021-2025/Year-1/Semester-3/COM107_Circuits_and_Devices/`, with any of these subfolders that have content:

- `Past-Papers/` — 空白往年试卷 | blank past papers (questions only)
- `Syllabus/` — 教学大纲、课程介绍 | syllabus, teaching plan, course intro
- `Notes/` — 笔记 | notes
- `Assignments/` — 作业题目 | assignment briefs
- `Labs/` — 实验手册、实验指导 | lab manuals and experiment sheets
- `Tutorials/` — 习题课题目 | tutorial question sheets
- `Handbooks/` — 官方指南、模板、评分标准 | official guidelines, templates, rubrics
- `Exam-Review/` — 复习重点 | revision summaries
- `Tips/` — 学习建议、实验经验 | study and lab tips
- `README.md`（可选 | optional）— 授课老师、考核比例、小贴士 | lecturer, assessment weighting, tips

## 如何使用 | How to use

- 先进 `EEE/`，选你那一届（入学年-毕业年），再按学年、学期找到课程文件夹。
- 每一届的培养方案可能不同（比如 Engineering Graphics：2021 入学的同学大一时是 COM204，2025 入学的则是大二的 EEE220），所以各届分开存放。找不到时请按课程名或课程代码全库搜索，别只按学年翻。
- 在 GitHub 页面按 `T` 可以直接搜文件名。
- 试卷只有题目，没有答案——自己做、和同学讨论、去问老师，才是正确打开方式。

- Start in `EEE/`, pick your cohort (intake-graduation year), then go by year and semester to the course folder.
- Curricula differ between cohorts (e.g. Engineering Graphics was COM204 in Year 1 for the 2021 intake but EEE220 in Year 2 for the 2025 intake), which is why cohorts are kept apart. If you can't find something, search the whole repo by course name or code instead of browsing by year only.
- Press `T` on the GitHub repo page to search file names.
- Papers contain questions only, no answers. Work them out yourself, discuss with classmates, ask your lecturer.
