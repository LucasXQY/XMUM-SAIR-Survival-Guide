欢迎来到厦马 EEE 生存指南！电子信息工程作为厦马课程强度较大、考试较为频繁、学习资源相对有限的专业之一，本仓库旨在为 XMUM 电子信息工程专业建立一个开放的学习资料共享平台。通过汇集同学们的学习笔记、实验经验、考试复习资料与重点总结，并整理教师教学风格与学习建议，实现资源互助与经验传承，帮助更多同学高效学习、顺利备考，取得好成绩，并为未来申请实习与心仪 offer 打下坚实基础。

Welcome to the XMUM EEE Survival Guide! Electrical & Electronic Engineering is one of the heaviest programmes at Xiamen University Malaysia: lots of courses, frequent exams, and not many shared resources. This repo is an open, student-run place to pool notes, lab experience, revision material and exam-prep summaries, plus honest notes on lecturers' teaching styles and study advice. The goal: help each other study smarter, get through exams with good grades, and build a solid base for internships and that dream offer.

## 初衷 | Why this exists

- 在厦马，往年试卷和笔记一直在私下小圈子里流传，甚至被拿来倒卖。
- 能拿到的同学复习有方向，没花钱、没门路的同学只能硬扛——同一门课，起跑线却不一样。
- 我们觉得学习资料不该是付费特权。这里的一切都免费、公开，任何人都能看、都能用。
- 教育公平，从一份公开的空白试卷开始。

- At XMUM, past papers and notes have long circulated in private circles, and some have even been sold between students.
- Students who have them know what to expect; students who did not (or could not) pay are left guessing. Same course, unequal starting line.
- We believe study material should not be a paid privilege. Everything here is free and open to everyone.
- Educational equity can start with something as simple as a public blank past paper.

## 如何使用 | How to use

- 先进 `EEE/`，再按学年、学期找到课程文件夹。
- 课程代码和所在学年以 2021 级培养方案为准，不同年级可能不一样（比如 Engineering Graphics：2021 级是大一的 COM204，2025 级变成了大二的 EEE220）。找不到时请按课程名或课程代码搜索，别只按学年翻。
- 在 GitHub 页面按 `T` 可以直接搜文件名。
- 试卷只有题目，没有答案——自己做、和同学讨论、去问老师，才是正确打开方式。

- Start in `EEE/`, then go by year and semester to the course folder.
- Course codes and years follow the 2021-intake curriculum; later intakes may differ (e.g. Engineering Graphics was COM204 in Year 1 for the 2021 intake but EEE220 in Year 2 for the 2025 intake). If you can't find something, search by course name or code instead of browsing by year only.
- Press `T` on the GitHub repo page to search file names.
- Papers contain questions only, no answers. Work them out yourself, discuss with classmates, ask your lecturer.
