# 机会 | Opportunities

网站上的[机会页](https://xmum-sair.github.io/opportunities/)是一面便签墙：科研、实习、全职、内推码、比赛活动、升学奖学金，同学和老师都能贴；旁边是同学写的经验帖（科研经历、实习、求职面经、升学申请、比赛……）。这个文件只放**维护者整理**的条目和一篇入门指南；同学自己发的帖子存在网站的服务里，不在仓库里。

The site's [Opportunities page](https://xmum-sair.github.io/opportunities/) is a wall of notes: research, internships, full-time jobs, referral codes, competitions and events, study and scholarships, posted by students and lecturers, next to experience posts written by students (research, internships, job hunting and interviews, grad school applications, competitions …). This file holds only the entries **curated by the maintainers** and a getting-started guide; posts made on the site live in the site's service, not in the repository.

## 怎么发布 | How to post

- 在机会页点「发布机会」或「分享经验」，填表提交即可，**不用登录**，每次过一次人机验证。
- 提交后先由 AI 做一次初审：没问题直接上墙；拿不准的交给维护者人工看，通常一两天内处理；明显的广告、诈骗（要交押金、刷单）、代写、他人隐私会被退回，并告诉你原因。
- 内推码直接写在「内推码」一栏，卡片上有一键复制；有截止日期的写上日期，过期后自动收进「已截止」。
- 只发你有权公开的信息：老师的招人信息要老师公开说过或同意公开；不要发别人的电话、学号。
- 卡片上有「举报」：被 3 个人举报的帖子会先隐藏，等维护者处理。想删掉自己发的帖子或更正，发邮件到 `xieqiyi@ieee.org`。

- On the Opportunities page, press "Post an opportunity" or "Share an experience" and submit the form: **no login**, one human check per post.
- An automatic first check reads every post: fine posts go up at once; posts it is unsure about wait for a maintainer, usually a day or two; obvious adverts, scams (deposits, order brushing), homework writing and other people's personal data are sent back with the reason.
- Put a referral code in the "Referral code" field: the card gets a copy button. Give a deadline if there is one; expired notes move to "Closed" by themselves.
- Only post what you may make public: a lecturer's opening must have been announced or approved by them; never post other people's phone numbers or student IDs.
- Every card has "Report": a post reported by three people is hidden until a maintainer looks. To remove or correct your own post, email `xieqiyi@ieee.org`.

## 科研入门：怎么找老师进组 | Getting started: joining a research group

这一篇在网站上置顶在经验帖的最前面。/ Pinned at the top of the experience posts on the site.

- **先做功课再敲门**：去老师的 Google Scholar 或学院主页看最近两三年的论文，知道他具体在做什么，再决定找谁。方向对不上，再热情也白搭。
- **什么时候找**：大二下到大三是最合适的时间。FYP 分配前先找到老师，FYP 就能顺着组里现有的课题做，不用从零开始。
- **第一封邮件怎么写**：用学校邮箱，标题写清楚「本科生想加入课题组」加专业年级。正文三段以内：你是谁（专业、年级、相关课程成绩或做过的项目）、为什么对他的方向感兴趣（具体到某篇论文或某个问题）、能投入多少时间、想以什么形式参与（FYP / 志愿者 / RA）。附上简历和 GitHub 链接。不要群发模板邮件。
- **没回复怎么办**：一周没回就礼貌追问一次；还是没回，就去办公室或下课后当面问，大部分老师并不介意。
- **进组前把约定说清楚**：每周大概要花多少时间、多久见一次面、最后要交出什么（代码、报告还是论文）。
- **进组后的日常**：前几周基本是读文献、把组里已有的代码跑通；之后是每周例会汇报进度。老师最看重的是稳定：每周都有进展，哪怕很小；遇到卡点提前说，比临时消失好得多。
- **留下能拿出手的东西**：代码放 GitHub、实验记录写清楚、报告按论文格式写。这些申请研究生、找实习时都能直接用，比成绩单更有说服力。
- **想要推荐信**：至少跟满一个学期、做出一点实际成果再开口，老师才有东西可写。
- **别硬撑**：发现方向不合适或者时间实在排不开，尽早坦诚说明、体面退出。老师见得多了，这远比半途消失留下的印象好。

- **Do your homework first**: check the lecturer's Google Scholar page or the school website for papers from the last two or three years, so you know what they actually work on before deciding whom to ask. Enthusiasm doesn't make up for a mismatched topic.
- **When to ask**: the second half of Year 2 through Year 3 is the sweet spot. Find a supervisor before FYP allocation and your FYP can grow out of the group's existing work instead of starting from zero.
- **The first email**: use your university email, with a subject line like "Undergraduate interested in joining your research group" plus your programme and year. Three short paragraphs at most: who you are (programme, year, relevant course results or projects), why their direction interests you (name a specific paper or problem), and how much time you can commit and in what form (FYP / volunteer / RA). Attach a CV and a GitHub link. Never mass-mail a template.
- **No reply?** Follow up politely after a week. Still nothing: drop by the office or catch them after class. Most lecturers don't mind.
- **Agree the terms before you join**: how many hours a week are expected, how often you meet and what the deliverable is (code, a report or a paper).
- **What day-to-day looks like**: the first few weeks are reading papers and getting the group's existing code to run; after that it's a weekly meeting where you report progress. What lecturers value most is consistency: some progress every week, however small, and flagging blockers early rather than going quiet.
- **Leave something you can show**: code on GitHub, clean experiment logs, a report written in paper format. These feed straight into graduate-school and internship applications and say more than a transcript does.
- **If you want a reference letter**: stay at least one semester and have something concrete done before you ask, so there is something to write about.
- **Don't struggle on in silence**: if the topic turns out not to fit or your timetable can't take it, say so early and leave properly. Lecturers have seen it before, and it leaves a far better impression than disappearing halfway.
