# Year-1 | 大一

大一是打地基的一年：数学、电路、编程基础轮番上阵，课多考试也多。先把基础打牢，后面会轻松很多。课程按 Semester-1/2/3 分文件夹存放。

Year 1 is all about foundations: maths, circuits and programming basics, with plenty of courses and exams. Get these solid and later years get a lot easier. Courses are stored under Semester-1/2/3.
