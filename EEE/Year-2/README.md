# Year-2 | 大二

大二开始进入专业核心课，信号、电子、电磁这些硬骨头陆续登场，实验和报告也多起来了。互相分享经验，一起啃下来。

Year 2 moves into the core of the programme: signals, electronics, electromagnetics and other tough ones, plus more labs and reports. Share what you learn and get through it together.
